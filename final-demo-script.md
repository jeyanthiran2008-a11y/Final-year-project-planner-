## Walkthrough
1. **Problem and target audience (45 sec):** Explain the project-planning and documentation problem for final-year CSE teams.
2. **Idea scorecard (60 sec):** Change one feasibility/impact/risk value and explain the weighted formula.
3. **Project plan (60 sec):** Enter team/project details and show the milestone plan.
4. **Report assistant (60 sec):** Paste your own notes, generate a draft, and explain why human verification is needed.
5. **Weekly automation (60 sec):** Paste the prepared notes, generate a status report, and point out any classification that needs correction.
6. **Readiness checklist (45 sec):** Tick a verified item and show the completion percentage.
7. **Export and limitations (45 sec):** Export data and explain that this is a local template-based app, not a fact-checking AI model.
