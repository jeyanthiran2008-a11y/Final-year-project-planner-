## Source-quality checks
- Prefer official university, standards-body, government, or peer-reviewed sources.
- Record author/organization, title, publication date, URL/DOI, and access date.
- Open the original source and verify that it supports the claim.
- Distinguish a source's findings from your own interpretation.
- Do not invent citations. Use the citation style required by your department.
