# Ethics, Academic Integrity, and AI Use

1. Follow the institution's rules for AI assistance and disclosure.
2. Do not submit AI-generated writing unchanged as your own work.
3. Use your own notes, design decisions, implementation, and measured results.
4. Verify every citation against the original source.
5. Never invent research, user feedback, test outcomes, or performance numbers.
6. Avoid uploading private student data or confidential project material to unapproved tools.
7. Record the tool, date, prompt, output used, changes made, and verification steps.
8. Explain the limitations of AI output, including hallucinations, bias, and outdated information.
9. Keep a clear distinction between source evidence, generated suggestions, and your team's conclusions.
10. Ask your supervisor when the permitted use of AI is unclear.
